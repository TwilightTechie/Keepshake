01 / THE HARDWARE

A tiny device.
A lot going on.

Inspired by the ease of a Whoop-style band. Built to help you remember, with a physical way to take it offline.

01 / CAPTURE

PDM microphone

A tiny acoustic opening is planned on the top shell. Voice activity detection is intended to capture speech rather than every second of your day.

02 / CONTROL

Physical switch

The reference design places a slide switch on the side. The product plan calls for a true hardware off path and a visible recording indicator.

03 / CONNECT

BLE + USB-C

The nRF52840 prototype sends audio to the phone over Bluetooth Low Energy; USB-C keeps charging straightforward.

04 / CARRY

Battery + strap

P0 is exploring protected 500 and 1100 mAh Li-Po options under the module. Fit, battery life, and enclosure details still need prototype testing.

Visual design study based on the supplied enclosure infographic and current project requirements. The band form, component layout, and final specifications may change.
